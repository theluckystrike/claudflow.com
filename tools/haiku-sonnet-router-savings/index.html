How this is computed

Formula: routed requests pay Haiku rates; a share of those fail the quality bar and are retried on Sonnet, paying both calls. Router overhead adds classifier input tokens to every request. Break-even retry rate is the point where routing stops saving money: retry_breakeven = (1 − cost_ratio × (1 − route)) where cost_ratio is Haiku cost as a share of Sonnet cost, adjusted for router overhead.
