The rollup model

cost per deliverable = (1 + f) × A × (1 − e + m·e) × (1 + r) + t · rate/60 · (1 + r), where f is the first-attempt failure rate, e the escalation share, m the escalation cost multiplier, r the rework rate, and t the human review minutes. Tokens are the receipt; cost per deliverable is the invoice.
